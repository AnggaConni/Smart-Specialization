How to interpret this feed

Discovery, not verification

01
Discover

GDELT finds article URLs matching configured topic queries in its recent monitoring window.

02
Check the original

Open the publisher page and verify the date, claims, geography, and whether the story is genuinely relevant.

03
Corroborate

Compare independent sources or official statistics before treating a story as evidence of an economic signal.

GDELT's source-country field describes the source's country metadata; it does not prove the story's event location. Duplicate syndication and noisy keyword matches can occur.
